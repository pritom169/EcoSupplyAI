# Supplier Code of Conduct

## Purpose

This Supplier Code of Conduct outlines the minimum standards expected of all suppliers, contractors, and business partners across our value chain. Compliance with this Code is a prerequisite for doing business with us and is subject to regular verification through audits and assessments.

## 1. Environmental Standards

- **Emissions Reduction**: Suppliers must measure, report, and actively reduce greenhouse gas emissions (Scope 1, 2, and 3). A documented decarbonisation plan with annual targets is required for Tier 1 suppliers.
- **Resource Efficiency**: Suppliers shall minimise waste generation, water consumption, and energy usage. Circular economy principles should be applied wherever feasible.
- **Pollution Prevention**: All operations must comply with applicable environmental laws. Suppliers must implement controls to prevent pollution of air, water, and soil.
- **Biodiversity**: Suppliers must assess and mitigate their impact on biodiversity and ecosystems, particularly in sourcing raw materials.

## 2. Labour & Human Rights

- **Fair Wages**: Workers must receive at least the legal minimum wage, with the aspiration toward a living wage. Overtime must be voluntary and compensated.
- **Working Hours**: Working hours must comply with local laws and not exceed 48 regular hours plus 12 overtime hours per week. Workers are entitled to at least one rest day per seven-day period.
- **No Forced Labour**: All forms of forced, bonded, or compulsory labour are strictly prohibited. Workers must be free to terminate employment with reasonable notice.
- **No Child Labour**: Suppliers must not employ anyone below the legal minimum working age or below 15 years, whichever is higher.
- **Non-Discrimination**: Employment decisions must be based on ability, not on race, gender, age, religion, disability, sexual orientation, or any other protected characteristic.
- **Freedom of Association**: Workers have the right to form and join trade unions and to bargain collectively without fear of retaliation.

## 3. Health & Safety

- **Safe Workplace**: Suppliers must provide a safe and healthy working environment, including proper ventilation, lighting, fire safety equipment, and emergency exits.
- **Training**: Workers must receive adequate safety training in a language they understand before commencing work and at regular intervals thereafter.
- **Incident Reporting**: All workplace accidents and near-misses must be recorded, investigated, and corrective actions implemented.

## 4. Governance & Ethics

- **Anti-Corruption**: Suppliers must not engage in bribery, corruption, extortion, or embezzlement. Adequate internal controls and reporting mechanisms must be in place.
- **Conflicts of Interest**: Business relationships must be transparent and free from undisclosed conflicts of interest.
- **Data Protection**: Personal data of employees, customers, and partners must be handled in compliance with applicable data protection regulations (e.g., GDPR).
- **Intellectual Property**: Suppliers must respect intellectual property rights and protect confidential information.

## 5. Monitoring & Compliance

- Suppliers agree to periodic audits (announced or unannounced) to verify compliance with this Code.
- Material non-compliance will result in a corrective action plan with defined timelines. Failure to remediate may result in termination of the business relationship.
- Suppliers must maintain documentation sufficient to demonstrate compliance for a minimum of five years.

## 6. Reporting Concerns

Suppliers and their workers may report concerns or violations confidentially through our ethics hotline or by emailing compliance@example.com. We operate a strict non-retaliation policy for good-faith reports.
