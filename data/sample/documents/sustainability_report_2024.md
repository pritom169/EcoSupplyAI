# Annual Sustainability Report 2024 - Executive Summary

## Overview

This report summarises our sustainability performance for the fiscal year 2024. We remain committed to embedding environmental, social, and governance (ESG) principles across our operations and supply chain, in alignment with the EU Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD) and the European Sustainability Reporting Standards (ESRS).

## Environmental Performance

| Metric                          | FY2023     | FY2024     | Change  |
|---------------------------------|------------|------------|---------|
| Total GHG Emissions (tCO2e)     | 84,200     | 78,500     | -6.8%   |
| Scope 1 Emissions (tCO2e)       | 12,400     | 11,200     | -9.7%   |
| Scope 2 Emissions (tCO2e)       | 18,600     | 16,800     | -9.7%   |
| Scope 3 Emissions (tCO2e)       | 53,200     | 50,500     | -5.1%   |
| Renewable Energy Share          | 52%        | 61%        | +9pp    |
| Waste Recycling Rate            | 68%        | 74%        | +6pp    |
| Water Consumption (m3)          | 1,240,000  | 1,180,000  | -4.8%   |

Our total greenhouse gas emissions declined by 6.8% year-on-year, driven by increased renewable energy procurement, fleet electrification, and supplier engagement programmes. Scope 3 emissions, which account for 64% of our total footprint, decreased by 5.1% as a result of targeted supply chain decarbonisation initiatives.

## Supply Chain Sustainability

- **Active Suppliers Assessed**: 247 out of 312 (79.2%)
- **Average Supplier ESG Score**: 72.4 / 100 (up from 68.1 in FY2023)
- **Suppliers with Corrective Action Plans**: 18 (7.3%)
- **Suppliers Terminated for Non-Compliance**: 3

We expanded our supplier assessment programme to cover 79% of active suppliers by spend. Eighteen suppliers were placed on corrective action plans following audits, and three supplier relationships were terminated due to persistent non-compliance with our Supplier Code of Conduct.

## Social Impact

- **Employee Satisfaction Score**: 4.2 / 5.0
- **Gender Diversity (Management)**: 41% women (up from 38%)
- **Lost Time Injury Rate**: 1.8 per million hours worked (down from 2.3)
- **Training Hours per Employee**: 42 hours (up from 36)
- **Living Wage Coverage**: 100% of direct employees; 87% of Tier 1 supplier workers

## Governance

- **Board ESG Oversight**: Dedicated Sustainability Committee established
- **Ethics Hotline Reports**: 14 reports received, 14 investigated, 12 resolved
- **Anti-Corruption Training**: 98% completion rate among employees
- **Data Protection Incidents**: 0 material breaches

## Outlook for 2025

Key priorities for the coming year include:
1. Achieving 70% renewable energy share across all operations
2. Extending supplier ESG assessments to 90% coverage by spend
3. Publishing our first CSRD-compliant sustainability statement
4. Implementing a digital battery passport for applicable product lines
5. Reducing Scope 3 emissions by a further 8% through supplier engagement
6. Launching a biodiversity impact assessment for high-risk sourcing regions

This report has been prepared in accordance with the ESRS and is subject to limited assurance by our independent auditor.
